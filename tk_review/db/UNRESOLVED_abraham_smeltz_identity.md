# Unresolved: Abraham 2024's unbound fractions may not be its own

**Status: open. Do not rely on either resolution until the Abraham full text
is read.**

## The observation

A literature fetch for Smeltz 2023's per-compound human plasma `fup` values
returned eleven numbers. All eleven are **identical, to the last digit**, to
the `fup` values this project records for Abraham 2024:

| | agent-reported Smeltz | this project's Abraham |
|---|---|---|
| PFBA | 0.0930 | 0.0930 |
| PFPeA | 0.0440 | 0.0440 |
| PFHxA | 0.0068 | 0.0068 |
| PFHpA | 0.0004 | 0.0004 |
| PFOA | 0.0010 | 0.0010 |
| PFNA | 0.0016 | 0.0016 |
| PFDA | 0.0027 | 0.0027 |
| PFUnDA | 0.0148 | 0.0148 |
| PFBS | 0.0046 | 0.0046 |
| PFHxS | 0.0009 | 0.0009 |
| PFOS | 0.0045 | 0.0045 |

Eleven independent agreements to two significant figures across values spanning
0.0004 to 0.093 cannot be coincidence between two separate measurements.

## The two explanations, and why neither is confirmed

**(a) Abraham 2024 took its unbound fractions from Smeltz 2023.** If so,
Abraham and Smeltz are ONE human series, not two, and this project's claim that
Abraham's T "needs no cross-study join" is wrong — the clearances would be
Abraham's and the binding EPA's.

**(b) The values are a fabrication.** The fetching agent stated plainly that the
per-compound numbers came from a model-extracted summary rather than verbatim
page text, and flagged internal inconsistencies in that summary (PFBS and PFOS
conflicting between its Tables 2 and 3; a miscount of how many compounds the
paper calls highly uncertain). A summarising model asked for PFAS unbound
fractions could have produced Abraham's values from elsewhere.

**Neither is verified.** A second fetch to establish Abraham's `fup` provenance
failed: the DOI redirected to an Elsevier link that returned only
"Redirecting", ScienceDirect returned 403, and PubMed returned a cookie
interstitial. No Methods sentence, no citation and no table was read.

## One piece of circumstantial support for (a)

Smeltz's abstract, read verbatim from PubMed, states that "binding was lower
for the PFCAs with a carbon chain length of >= 11". In the disputed list,
PFUnDA (C11) is 0.0148 — **less bound than PFDA (C10) at 0.0027**, exactly the
pattern Smeltz describes. That same uptick is the one this review previously
called unsupportable by any binding mechanism. It is suggestive, not decisive.

## What this changes now

- **Smeltz 2023 is NOT ingested.** No value from that fetch enters the record.
- The report's statement that Abraham's T needs no cross-study join is
  **qualified**, not deleted: the GFR term is certainly the subject's own
  (measured creatinine clearance, 113.7 mL/min), and the clearances are
  certainly measured in that subject. Only the binding term is in doubt.
- If (a) holds, the human series count is four, not five: Ryu, Argoul, Fischer,
  and Smeltz-via-Abraham.

## What would resolve it

One reading of Abraham 2024 (Environ Int 193:109047) — the Methods sentence
describing how `fup` was obtained, and whether a citation is attached to it.
The full text is paywalled from this session.
