THE UNKNOWN: THE HUMAN TUBULAR FACTOR T = CL / (f_u * GFR)
==============================================================================
  T < 1 is net reabsorption, T = 1 filtration alone, T > 1 net
  secretion. Every model below differs ONLY in where T comes from.

  cmpd      nC         T      1/T   reading
  PFBA       4    0.0372     26.9   strong reabsorption
  PFBS       4    0.0200     50.0   strong reabsorption
  PFDA      10    0.0932     10.7   strong reabsorption
  PFDoDA    12    3.0832      0.3   net secretion
  PFHpA      7    0.0511     19.6   strong reabsorption
  PFHxS      6    0.0297     33.7   strong reabsorption
  PFNA       9    0.0137     72.9   strong reabsorption
  PFOA       8    0.0066    151.0   strong reabsorption
  PFOS       8    0.0450     22.2   strong reabsorption

  Geometric mean T = 0.0479  (1/T = 20.9)
  Full range spans 466-fold.
  1 compound(s) show net secretion: PFDoDA
  Excluding it, T spans 14.1-fold (0.0066 to 0.0932),
  which is the irreducible error of any single-constant T model.

  WATCH PFDoDA. Its T of 3.08 is the only value above 1,
  it is the longest chain in the set, and its half-life
  (295 d) is SHORTER than PFDA's (862 d),
  breaking the chain-length trend. It drives several results
  below and is flagged wherever it does.

MODEL 1  MECHANISTIC TK -- f_u, V_d, GFR measured; T the unknown
==============================================================================
  model                                     n  k    median     worst
  M1a: T=1 (filtration alone)               9  0     26.9x    151.0x
  M1b: T = fitted constant                  9  1      2.1x    108.4x
  M1c: log T = a + b*nC                     9  2      2.2x     89.4x
  M1d: f_u from albumin K_a, n=1, T fitted  9  1      5.1x     19.3x
  M1d: f_u from albumin K_a, n=2, T fitted  9  1      5.1x     16.1x
  M1d: f_u from albumin K_a, n=3, T fitted  9  1      5.1x     15.0x

  M1a FAILS AS EXPECTED: median 26.9x, worst 151x, and it UNDER-predicts in
  8 of 9 compounds. Assuming T=1 credits the kidney with
  clearing everything it filters, so the predicted half-life is
  too short by exactly the factor the tubule reabsorbs.

  M1b, ONE PARAMETER, CUTS THE MEDIAN TO 2.1x. That single
  constant is doing the work of the entire tubular mechanism, and
  what it leaves behind is the compound-to-compound spread in T.

  M1c LETS T TRACK CHAIN LENGTH AND IS WORSE (2.2x vs 2.1x).
  With one secreting outlier at the long-chain end, a linear
  trend in log T is fitted largely to that one point.

  M1d REPLACES MEASURED f_u WITH ALBUMIN BINDING on the 9 compounds
  with a human K_a. Best is n=2 at 5.1x median, 16x worst,
  against M1b's 2.1x on the full set -- so binding is a
  usable stand-in for a measured f_u, not an improvement on it.

  THE THREE MEDIANS AGREE TO WITHIN 0.1% (5.120x to 5.126x)
  AND THAT IS NOT A COINCIDENCE.
  With tight binding, n[Alb]K_a >> 1 so f_u ~ 1/(n[Alb]K_a),
  i.e. f_u is proportional to 1/n. The fitted T is
  proportional to 1/f_u and therefore to n, so n cancels
  exactly in the product f_u*T. It survives only through the
  weakly bound short chains, where n[Alb]K_a is not >> 1.
  THE DATA CONFIRM EXACTLY THAT PATTERN. Going n=1 -> n=3,
  PFBA -- the weakest binder in the set -- moves 19.3x to 15.0x
  (1.29-fold), while NO OTHER COMPOUND MOVES MORE THAN 4.9%
  (PFDoDA), and the worst case overall shifts only 19.3x to 15.0x.
  The number of binding sites is therefore NOT IDENTIFIABLE
  from these data once T is fitted.

SENSITIVITY: WHICH f_u STUDY, ON THE PARAMETER-FREE MODEL
==============================================================================
  The compilation uses Ryu 2024, while db/human_fu_recommended.csv
  names Smeltz 2023 as the default human series. Both are run,
  with Argoul and Fischer alongside.

  f_u source                n    median     worst  direction
  Smeltz 2023 (UC)          8     46.9x    132.9x  under
  Ryu 2024                  8     33.7x    151.0x  under
  Argoul 2025               6     66.0x    104.8x  under
  Fischer 2024              6     22.5x     80.8x  under

    Smeltz 2023 (UC): no value for PFDoDA
    Ryu 2024: no value for PFDoDA
    Argoul 2025: no value for PFBA, PFDoDA, PFHpA
    Fischer 2024: no value for PFBA, PFBS, PFDoDA
    (PFDoDA is absent from human_fu_comparison.csv entirely, so
     every row above is scored without it.)

  THE CONCLUSION DOES NOT DEPEND ON THE CHOICE. Every source
  leaves a large shortfall in the same direction, because f_u
  varies 2- to 10-fold between studies while the shortfall is
  roughly 30-fold. The f_u choice is not what is missing.

THE SAME COMPARISON WITH PFDoDA DROPPED
==============================================================================
  Not to improve the numbers, but to find out which conclusions
  rest on that one compound. The 8 remaining compounds are all
  reabsorptive, which is the regime the mechanistic models assume.

  model                                       median     worst
  M1b: T = fitted constant                      2.1x      9.3x
  M1c: log T = a + b*nC                         2.2x     11.4x
  QSAR log t½ ~ nC + nC^2                       6.9x     15.1x
  QSAR log t½ ~ nC + nC^2 + head                3.5x      9.0x
  QSAR log t½ ~ nC                              3.5x     63.4x
  M1d: f_u from albumin K_a, n=2, T fitted      5.1x     16.1x
  M1d: f_u from albumin K_a, n=3, T fitted      5.1x     15.0x
  M1d: f_u from albumin K_a, n=1, T fitted      5.1x     19.3x
  QSAR log t½ ~ nC + head                       5.5x     37.9x
  M1a: T=1 (filtration alone)                  33.7x    151.0x

  Best median now M1b: T = fitted constant at 2.1x;
  best worst case QSAR log t½ ~ nC + nC^2 + head at 9.0x.
  On the full set the median winner was M1b: T = fitted constant.
  WHETHER THE RANKING SURVIVES DROPPING ONE COMPOUND OUT OF NINE
  IS THE REAL TEST, and with 9 compounds it is a weak one either
  way. No model here is established; they are ranked, provisionally.

  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SETTLE. Both models are now fitted
  to human half-lives, so neither can be carried to a species
  whose half-life is unknown -- the usual reason for wanting one.
  The mechanistic models earn something the QSAR cannot: their
  one parameter IS the tubular factor, so a fitted T is a
  physiological quantity that can be compared against transporter
  data. The QSAR's coefficients are not interpretable that way.
