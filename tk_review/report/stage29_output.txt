PART 1  PFDoDA AT SOURCE: THE HALF-LIFE IS REAL, MY T WAS NOT
==============================================================================
  Abraham 2024 Table 3:  t½ = 295 d  (95% CI 274-321)
                         V_d = 354 mL/kg (342-368)
  n = 1 volunteer, single oral dose, 450 days of sampling.

  HOW MUCH HUMAN PFDoDA HALF-LIFE DATA EXISTS: THIS, AND NOTHING
  ELSE. A PubMed search for perfluorododecanoic acid
  toxicokinetics returns 10 records, none of them a human
  half-life study; the hits are rat brain distribution, RIVM rat
  modelling, minipig PK and fish bioconcentration. So the value
  is a single measurement in a single person and cannot be
  cross-checked against an independent study.

  IS IT AN OUTLIER? NO -- IT IS THE END OF A TREND.
    PFOA 2011d  ->  PFNA 1305d  ->  PFDA 862d  ->  PFUnDA 584d  ->  PFDoDA 295d
  Five consecutive chain lengths, monotonically decreasing after
  PFOA. A single bad number cannot produce a monotone run of four
  decreases, so the pattern is a property of the data.

  WHAT IS WRONG IS THE ROUTE ATTRIBUTION. Abraham Table 4:
  cmpd        CL_uri   CL_tot   CL_fec  fecal %   non-renal evidence
  PFBA           1.0     1.24     n.d.        -   urinary route measured
  PFBS         0.067    0.107     n.d.        -   urinary route measured
  PFHxS         n.d.    0.003     n.d.        -   no route detected either way
  PFHpA        0.023    0.033     n.d.        -   urinary route measured
  PFOA          n.d.   0.0024   0.0004      17%   faecal route measured
  PFOS          n.d.    0.005     n.d.        -   no route detected either way
  PFNA          n.d.   0.0038   0.0022      58%   faecal route measured
  PFDA          n.d.   0.0084   0.0051      61%   faecal route measured
  PFDoDA        n.d.   0.0474   0.0286      60%   faecal route measured
  PFUnDA        n.d.   0.0163   0.0098      60%   faecal route measured

  Urinary clearance is NOT DETECTED for all five long-chain PFCAs
  and for both long-chain PFSAs. The authors say so plainly: they
  could not detect long-chain PFAS in urine at these doses.

  T from total clearance credits the kidney with the faecal route
  as well. Using the faecal FRACTION (dimensionless, so no body-
  weight conversion is needed) gives a renal-only upper bound:
      CL_renal <= CL_total * (1 - CL_fec/CL_tot)

  PFDoDA: T falls from 3.083 to 1.223.
  It is STILL above 1 (PFDoDA), so the correction

  A PREDICTION OF MINE THAT THE NUMBERS DID NOT BEAR OUT. Because
  T is now derived with the same f_u the model divides by, the
  absolute LEVEL of f_u cancels in M1b, so I expected M1b to be
  far less sensitive to the choice than M1a. It is not:
      M1a medians span 2.9-fold across the options
      M1b medians span 2.6-fold
  Comparable. The reason is that the four studies disagree about
  the SHAPE of f_u across compounds, not only its level -- Smeltz
  and Ryu differ 6.0-fold on PFOS but only 1.3-fold on PFHxS. The
  level cancels; the shape is what M1b depends on, and the
  disagreement lives there too. So the f_u choice is not made
  harmless by fitting T.

  RECOMMENDATION -- AND THE TWO LINES OF EVIDENCE DO NOT AGREE,
  SO BOTH ARE STATED.
    Best median on the common set is Argoul 2025 (1.3x), but it
    reports only 6 of the 9 compounds and has the WORST M1a of all
    (66.0x), so its edge rests on the fewest compounds.

    PRIMARY: Smeltz 2023. Not because it wins the median -- it is
    second at 1.6x -- but because it has the best worst case
    (2.6x), covers 8 of 9 compounds, is the largest series (67 PFAS),
    and is the f_u Abraham 2024 used in its own Table 4. That last
    point is the one that is not a fit statistic: it makes the
    half-life, V_d and f_u a single published pairing.

    SENSITIVITY: the consensus geometric mean (1.7x median, 3.2x worst),
    which covers all 9 and sits mid-range, carried with the per-
    compound spread (up to 11.1-fold on PFHpA) from
    db/human_fu_consensus.csv.

    CAVEAT TO STATE IN THE PAPER: Smeltz has no PFDoDA, so that
    compound takes Ryu's value under any choice. The f_u column is
    therefore not from a single source and should not be presented
    as one.
