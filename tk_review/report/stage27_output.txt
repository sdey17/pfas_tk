==============================================================================
BEFORE ANY MODEL: SCREENING THE MEASURED RENAL CLEARANCES
==============================================================================
  CL_R is one component of CL_tot, so CL_R > CL_tot is impossible,
  not merely discrepant. Each measured value is therefore checked
  against the total clearance it would be paired with.

  7 of 26 values FAIL:
    human PFHpA    CL_R   0.61 vs CL_tot 0.584    = 1.04x   Zhang 2013
    human PFHpA    CL_R  0.674 vs CL_tot 0.584    = 1.15x   Fujii 2015
    human PFNA     CL_R  0.094 vs CL_tot 0.0659   = 1.43x   Zhang 2013
    human PFNA     CL_R   0.15 vs CL_tot 0.0659   = 2.28x   Zhang 2013
    human PFOA     CL_R  0.044 vs CL_tot 0.0417   = 1.05x   Fujii 2015
    human PFOA     CL_R   0.19 vs CL_tot 0.0417   = 4.56x   Zhang 2013
    human PFOA     CL_R   0.77 vs CL_tot 0.0417   = 18.47x   Zhang 2013

  These are not dropped for being inconvenient. A renal clearance
  above total clearance means the two numbers come from
  populations with different half-lives -- Zhang 2013 pairs its
  high PFOA clearance with a ~1.2-year half-life in young women,
  against the ~2-4 years in the compilation -- so the product of
  one study's CL_R and another's V_d has no referent. WHICH SIDE
  IS WRONG IS NOT SETTLED HERE; only the pairing is void.

  ONE EXCEPTION, DELIBERATE. Tier 3 still uses Fujii's PFOA renal
  FRACTION (0.044/0.096 = 45.8%) even though its absolute CL_R was
  just voided. A fraction is dimensionless and internally
  consistent within one study, so it survives a disagreement about
  the absolute scale that the raw clearance does not.

==============================================================================
AUDIT: IS TIER 2's ERROR REALLY 1/f_renal?
==============================================================================
  The claim is arithmetic, so it is testable. If tier 2 omits every
  non-renal route then its fold error must equal CL_tot/CL_R. Both
  sides are computed independently below.

                   tier 2 fold  CL_tot/CL_R   agree
  human PFDA             4.01x        4.01x     yes
  human PFDoDA         166.40x      166.40x     yes
  human PFHpA            1.42x        1.42x     yes
  human PFHxS            2.63x        2.63x     yes
  human PFNA             1.73x        1.73x     yes
  human PFOA             1.40x        1.39x     yes
  human PFOS             4.40x        4.40x     yes
  rat   PFHxS            8.88x        8.88x     yes
  rat   PFOA            11.13x       11.12x     yes
  rat   PFOS             4.81x        4.81x     yes

  Every row agrees. Tier 2's error is not a modelling failure;
  it IS the reciprocal of the renal share, measured two ways.

  The worst row, human PFDoDA at 166x, is therefore not an outlier in the model.
  It says that compound's renal clearance is 1/166 of its total.

  READING TIER 2. It uses a renal clearance and nothing about the
  other routes, so it must OVER-predict the half-life by exactly
  1/f_renal. The over-prediction is not an error in the model; it
  is a measurement of how much elimination is non-renal.

==============================================================================
A CONFLICT TIER 3 FORCES INTO THE OPEN: THE RAT RENAL FRACTION
==============================================================================
  human PFOA: 25.1% to 45.8%  (1.8-fold apart)
        25.1%   Harada 2005 Table 5, male 61 kg
        45.8%   Fujii 2015 Table 2, urinary 0.044 / total 0.096
  macaque PFOA: 40.4% to 41.5%  (1.0-fold apart)
        40.4%   Harada 2005 Table 5, male Japanese macaque
        41.5%   Harada 2005 Table 5, female
  rat PFOA: 9.0% to 91.4%  (10.2-fold apart)
         9.0%   Kim 2016 Table 4 (male), CL_R/CL_tot
        47.2%   Harada 2005 Table 5, female Wistar
        91.4%   Harada 2005 Table 5, male Wistar

  The rat PFOA figure is the one that matters. Harada 2005 Table 5
  has renal clearance accounting for essentially all of it, which
  is the basis for the familiar claim that the rat clears PFOA
  renally while the human does not. Kim 2016 reports CL_R and
  CL_tot IN THE SAME TABLE, on the same animals, and gets 9%.
  Both cannot be right, and tier 3 for the rat swings by that
  same factor depending on which is used. This is a disagreement
  between two primary sources, not a modelling choice, and it is
  recorded rather than resolved.

  THE COMPARISON IS NOT LIKE FOR LIKE AND SHOULD NOT BE READ AS
  ONE. The TK tiers spend no degrees of freedom and need three to
  five measured inputs per compound; the QSAR spends two to four
  and needs a structural formula. A QSAR that wins on fold error
  has still been calibrated on the very half-lives it predicts,
  so it cannot be carried to a species whose half-lives are the
  unknown -- which is the usual reason for wanting the model.
