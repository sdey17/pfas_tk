SUBJECT:  Two questions about the cellular concentration data in Rosenmai et al. 2017

---

Dear Dr Rosenmai and colleagues,

I am re-analysing published in vitro PFAS uptake data to benchmark two
mass-balance models (Armitage et al. 2021; Qin et al. 2023), and your 2017
HepG2 dataset is one of the most complete available. Thank you for reporting
both the uptake percentages and the per-protein concentrations — very few
studies give both.

I am having trouble reconciling the two, and I think it comes down to a unit
that I may simply be misreading.

Taking PFOA at 100 uM: Table 1 gives a cellular uptake of 0.33% of the moles
added, and Figure 2A shows approximately 525. If Figure 2A is in nmol/mg
protein, then with roughly 0.5 ng protein per HepG2 cell and the 3 pooled
12-well wells described in section 2.5, the cells would hold about 47% of the
added PFOA rather than 0.33%. Reading Figure 2A as ng/mg protein instead
brings the two into agreement to within about a factor of two. I notice the
Abstract gives the range as "4-2500 ng mg-1 protein" while Results 3.3 gives
the same range as nmol mg-1 protein, so I suspect one of the two is a
typographical slip — but I cannot tell from the paper which.

Two pieces of information would settle it, and would let me use your data
properly:

  1. What was the working volume of exposure medium per well in the 12-well
     plates used for the chemical analysis (section 2.5)?

  2. What was the measured total protein per sample (or per well) in those
     experiments? Supplementary Figure S2 reports protein per well in ng/cm2,
     which for a 3.5 cm2 well works out at a few micrograms, and I may be
     misreading the axis.

If it is easier to send the underlying numbers behind Figure 2A and Figure S2
than to answer in prose, that would be very welcome, and I am happy to share
what I find back with you.
