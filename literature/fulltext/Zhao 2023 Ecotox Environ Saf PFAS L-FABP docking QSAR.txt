                                                            environmental media and human serum, but their safety assessment remains challenging. PFASs may accumulate

                                                            machine learning model-based quantitative structure-activity relationship (QSAR) for efficient prediction of
                                                            potentially hazardous PFASs. Further Bayesian Kernel Machine Regression (BKMR) model disclosed flexibility as
                                                            the determinant molecular property on PFASs-induced hepatotoxicity. It can influence affinity of PFASs to target
                                                            protein through affecting binding conformations directly (individual effect) as well as integrating with other

                                                            development of PFASs.

2.4. Correlation analysis

   In order to assess the credibility of molecular docking, a regression
analysis was conducted using IBM SPSS Statistics 25. Dissociation con­
stants (Kd) of PFASs towards L-FABP from previous studies (Yang et al.,
2020) and the binding energy obtained from molecular docking were
used as the independent and dependent variables, respectively. The
Pearson Correlation Coefficient (r) was selected to evaluate the degree of
explanation of independent variable towards dependent variable,
namely Kd and binding energy.
